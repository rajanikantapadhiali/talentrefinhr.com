What We Do

End-to-End HR Services

From hiring your first employee to managing a full workforce — we handle every piece of the HR puzzle.

🎯

Talent Acquisition & Recruitment

End-to-end hiring support — job analysis, sourcing, screening, interviewing, and onboarding for all levels and functions.

💰

Payroll Processing

Accurate, on-time payroll with full statutory compliance — PF, ESI, TDS, PT — with detailed payslips and MIS reports.

📋

HR Compliance & Labour Law

Stay audit-ready with expert guidance on PF, ESI, Shops & Establishments, Factories Act, and all applicable labour regulations.

📈

Performance Management

Design and implement KRA/KPI frameworks, appraisal cycles, and feedback systems that drive employee performance.

🎓

Training & Development

Customised learning programmes — leadership development, soft skills, compliance training, and new-hire orientation.

📄

HR Policy & Documentation

Drafting and reviewing employee handbooks, offer letters, appointment orders, NDAs, and complete HR policy manuals.

🤝

Employee Relations

Grievance handling, disciplinary processes, POSH compliance, and building a healthy, engaged workplace culture.

🏢

HR Outsourcing (HRO)

Complete outsourced HR department for SMEs — we act as your HR team, available on a retainer or project basis.

🔍

Background Verification

Thorough pre-employment verification — education, employment history, criminal records, and reference checks.
